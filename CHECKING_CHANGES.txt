1. ISSUES DISCOVERED
================================================================================
[Security & Credential Management]
- A hardcoded live GNews API key ('71e3406559b0c50de45c7da3856f0b9e') was exposed directly in `test_pipeline.py`.
- Authentication in `services/auth.py` and `services/db.py` stored and checked passwords as plaintext, despite `bcrypt` being listed in `requirements.txt`.
- `.env.example` was minimal and lacked documentation for Langfuse keys and demo flags.

[Risk Scoring & Credibility Coupling]
- In `agents/risk_agent.py`, risk was hardcoded to arbitrary values (85, 90, 50) based on string matching.
- In `agents/orchestrator.py`, the risk score was overridden with hardcoded numbers, ignoring the agent.
- Physical disaster severity (e.g., Earthquake vs minor flood) was completely conflated with misinformation falsehood risk.
- Risk scores lacked mathematical explainability and diagnostic factor breakdowns.

[Verification & Journalistic Evidence Standards]
- In `agents/verification_agent.py`, credibility scores were generated using `random.randint(...)`, producing non-deterministic and unscientific ratings.
- Verification verdicts lacked cautious journalistic statuses (Supported, Contradicted, Unverified, Conflicting).
- News reports were treated as automatic proof rather than evidence under active investigation.

[Simulation Transparency]
- Mock and fallback data in `services/gnews.py` and `services/db.py` were not clearly distinguished from real incidents.
- No clear UI indicator existed to inform authorities or citizens when external news APIs were in demo/fallback mode.

[Entity Extraction & Fallback Parsing]
- In `agents/extraction_agent.py`, non-crisis text like "Random nonsense text with no crisis" falsely extracted the word "Random" as a geographic location and marked the event as "Incident".
- Fallback regex parsing had poor handling for prepositional location phrases ("in <City>", "near <City>").

[Duplicate Detection]
- Although referenced in the user specifications, duplicate detection was completely absent from the codebase.
- No mechanism existed to identify viral misinformation claims being submitted repeatedly across communities.

[Incomplete Agents]
- `agents/summary_agent.py` was a stub returning a placeholder string ("Placeholder summary.").
- `agents/action_agent.py` was hardcoded to three generic one-line strings and failed to separate citizen safety guidance from civil authority operational directives.

[UI & Theme Deficiencies]
- In `components/charts.py`, Plotly charts set `font_color='#fff'`, making chart text nearly invisible against the light theme background.
- Charts and badge components lacked support for cautious verification statuses.

[OCR Engine Availability]
- On Windows systems without Tesseract installed, `services/ocr.py` silently failed without diagnostics.
- No fallback existed to transcribe text using multimodal vision LLMs.

[Platform Compatibility]
- Test runners and logging contained Unicode symbols (e.g. \u2713, \u2705) that crashed under Windows default `cp1252` console encoding with `UnicodeEncodeError`.
